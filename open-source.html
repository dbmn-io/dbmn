Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature, with no limits and no end date.

Do I need an account?

Not in VS Code. A free DBMN account keeps your endpoints and environments in one place, and is needed for the web app and Puppy School.

What does AGPL-3.0 mean for me?

Use Dobermann freely, at home or at work. If you change it and offer it to other people, share your changes under the same terms.

Can I use the names?

The code is open. The names DBMN and Dobermann and the logo are trademarks: a fork needs its own name.

What does it send home?

Usage counts are optional: on when you sign in, with one switch to turn them off. Signed out, nothing is sent. Never your data. See Data Transparency.

How does DBMN make money?

From optional hosted services. The app itself stays free and open.

I already pay for DBMN

Nothing changes without a word from us first. Manage your subscription from your Account, or email hello@dbmn.io.
